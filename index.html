03
UX Research & Design Expanded
Council on Foreign Relations

Leading stakeholder research and design synthesis for CFR's platform redesign

The Challenge

CFR was preparing to redesign its entire digital estate but critical decisions about information architecture and user experience were driven by internal assumptions rather than user evidence. The stakes: a multi-year investment with 1.5M+ monthly pageviews at risk.

My Approach

I coordinated a comprehensive research program spanning 15+ stakeholder and member interviews with vendor Upstatement, organized findings across audience segments — members, fellows, editorial staff, and leadership — and translated insights into actionable product requirements. I served as bridge between Figma design sprint reviews and internal teams, providing annotated feedback to ensure editorial and brand alignment before build.

04
Platform Migration & Redesign New
Council on Foreign Relations

Leading CFR.org's full redesign and migration to WordPress VIP

The Challenge

CFR's flagship website had relied on Drupal for over a decade. A comprehensive redesign and migration to WordPress VIP was mandated — covering the main site, Member Services Portal, Global Conflict Tracker, and 17+ microsites — requiring coordination across an external vendor, engineering, QA, editorial, and IT simultaneously.

My Approach

As Delivery Manager and Lead Scrum Master at CFR, I co-authored the RACI framework and managed a phased sequential launch strategy — progressively migrating content types from Drupal to WordPress VIP to minimize risk. I tracked blocker tickets weekly, maintained the Confluence launch plan, and authored weekly release notes keeping all stakeholders aligned through an 18-month engagement.

Outcome
17+Sites migrated to WordPress VIP
23%Of pageviews on new platform before full cutover — zero major bugs
