Agile Transformation

Rebuilding delivery velocity across a cross-functional digital team

Scaling content operations through org-wide CMS training

UX Research
Council on Foreign Relations

Using UX research to shape a new digital platform from the ground up

The organization needed to build new digital platforms but lacked structured user insight. Product decisions were being made on assumptions rather than evidence, creating risk for a large-scale investment.

I spearheaded UX research initiatives — designing research protocols, synthesizing findings, and translating user insights into actionable product requirements. I used tools including Google Analytics, Amplitude, and Parse.ly to layer quantitative data on top of qualitative research.
